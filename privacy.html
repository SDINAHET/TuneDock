TuneDock for Spotify Privacy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

TuneDock does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no server, advertising or analytics.

Data used locally

TuneDock reads visible Spotify Web player information only to display and control playback requested by the user.

Chrome storage

Preferences and widget settings are stored locally in Chrome. No Spotify credentials or OAuth token are stored.

Optional widget

HTTPS page access is requested only when the user enables the widget. The widget displays TuneDock controls and does not read page content, forms or browsing history.

Third-party services

TuneDock communicates only with Spotify Web over HTTPS to perform requested actions. Spotify use remains subject to Spotify's terms.

Optional lyrics search

After an explicit click, TuneDock can open a Google search containing the title and artist. It does not retrieve, store or reproduce lyrics.
